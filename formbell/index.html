Formbell

Send the right email when a Google Form response comes in.

Formbell is a Google Forms add-on. You set a rule, such as "if the answer to this question is X, email this person", and Formbell sends a personalized email when a matching response arrives.

Formbell is preparing for review on the Google Workspace Marketplace. It is not listed yet.

What you get

  • Conditional rules. Match on a question's answer (equals, contains, not empty, one of a list) and send to a fixed address or to an email from the response.
  • Personalized emails. Put response values into the subject and body. Basic HTML is supported.
  • Test before it goes live. Preview a recent response and send a test to yourself.
  • A send log you can read. Each send shows one of three states: Google accepted the send request, failed (with a reason), or result unknown. "Accepted" means Google took the request. It does not confirm inbox delivery, and we say so.
  • Resend when needed. Pick a failed or unknown entry and send it again. Resends are labeled.
  • Your Google sending limits, shown plainly. Formbell shows Google's remaining recipient count and how many recipients it requested today.

Prices and the free limit are shown inside the add-on before you pay. You can cancel any time from the billing portal linked in your purchase receipt email.

Your data

  • Formbell runs inside your Google account. Form responses are processed there and are not sent to our servers.
  • Rules and the send log are stored with your form. The log keeps up to the latest 200 entries per form for 30 days, and you can delete everything for a form at any time.
  • To check a Pro license, Formbell sends only the license key to our payment provider. It never sends response content or recipient addresses.

Full details: Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Honest limits

  • Google limits how many emails a script can send per day. Formbell works within those limits and does not promise unlimited sending.
  • Responses that wait more than 48 hours without being processed are not sent automatically. They stay in the log so you can send them yourself.
