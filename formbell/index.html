Google Forms add-on

Email the right person when a form answer matches

You pick a question and an answer, and choose who gets the email. When a matching response arrives, Formbell sends it automatically from your Google account.

Install FormbellFrom Google Workspace Marketplace. Conditional rules are included in the free plan.

What it does

You write rules like "if Request type is Sales, email the sales team." Each rule checks one question with is, contains, is not empty or is any of. It sends to a fixed address, or to an email address from the response.

You can put answers into the subject and the message, and use basic HTML. You can preview the email with a recent response, and send yourself a test.

The send log shows Sent, Failed (with a reason when one is available) or Unknown for each recorded send, and Processing while a send is still in progress. Sent means Google accepted the email for sending. It doesn't tell you whether the email reached the inbox.

You can review and resend eligible failed or unknown entries, and resends are labeled. Formbell also shows Google's remaining recipient count and how many recipients it requested today.

Your data

Form responses are processed in Google Apps Script under your Google account, and are not sent to Lazy Labs servers. Rule emails go to the recipients you choose, and test emails go only to you. Operational diagnostics in Google logs may be visible to Lazy Labs, as the Privacy Policy explains.

Rules and the send log are stored with your form. Each form keeps its latest 200 log entries, and you can view or resend entries from the last 30 days, whichever limit comes first. Expired entries are deleted the next time Formbell runs. You can delete a form's Formbell rules and log from that form.

To check a Pro license, Formbell sends the license key and activation information to Dodo Payments. It never sends response content or recipient addresses.

Limits

Google limits how many emails a script can send each day. Formbell works within those limits and doesn't promise unlimited sending.

Responses that Formbell sees more than 48 hours after they were submitted aren't sent automatically. You may still be able to resend eligible entries by hand while they are in the log.
