Formbell

Send the right email when a Google Form response comes in.

Formbell is a Google Forms add-on. You set a rule, such as "if the answer to this question is X, email this person", and Formbell sends a personalized email when a matching response arrives.

Install from Google Workspace Marketplace

Free plan included. Pro is optional.

What you get

  • Conditional rules. Match on a question's answer (equals, contains, not empty, one of a list) and send to a fixed address or to an email from the response.
  • Personalized emails. Put response values into the subject and body. Basic HTML is supported.
  • Preview and test. Preview a recent response and send a test to yourself.
  • A send log you can read. The send log shows Sent, Failed (with a reason), or Unknown. A send still in progress appears as Processing. "Sent" means Google accepted the email for sending. It does not confirm inbox delivery, and Formbell says so.
  • Resend when needed. Pick a failed or unknown entry and send it again. Resends are labeled.
  • Your Google sending limits, shown plainly. Formbell shows Google's remaining recipient count and how many recipients it requested today.

Your data

  • Form responses are processed in Google Apps Script under your Google account and are not sent to Lazy Labs servers. Rule emails go to the recipients you choose, and test emails go only to your own address. Operational diagnostics in Google logs may be accessible to Lazy Labs, as the Privacy Policy explains.
  • Rules and the send log are stored with your form. Each form shares its latest 200 log entries. Entries from the last 30 days can be viewed or resent, whichever limit is reached first. Expired entries are deleted the next time Formbell runs. You can delete a form's shared Formbell rules and log from that form.
  • To check a Pro license, Formbell sends the license key and activation information to Dodo Payments. It never sends response content or recipient addresses.

Full details: Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Honest limits

  • Google limits how many emails a script can send per day. Formbell works within those limits and does not promise unlimited sending.
  • Responses observed more than 48 hours after submission are not sent automatically. Retained eligible entries may be available for manual resend.
