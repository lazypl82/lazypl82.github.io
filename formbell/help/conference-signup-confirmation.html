For teachers

Confirm conference sign-ups with the time each family picked

Families sign up in a Google Forms™ form. Each one gets an email that names their child and the slot they chose, so they don't have to remember it or ask again.

You need one Formbell rule, so the free plan is enough. If your school manages your Google account, your administrator may decide which add-ons you can install.

The form

Make a short form, or copy last term's, with these questions:

  • Student nameshort answer, required
  • Time slotmultiple choice or dropdown, required
  • Attendingmultiple choice, required: Yes, No
  • Emailshort answer with email validation, only if you don't collect email addresses

Most families won't have a school Google account. If your form asks people to sign in, or only takes answers from your school's accounts, change that in the form's settings before you share the link.

The rule

When Attending is Yes, email the person who filled in the form.

If the form collects email addresses, choose Email collected by Google Forms. Otherwise choose your Email question.

For the subject, try "Your conference time for" followed by the Student name answer. In the message, use Insert answer to add the student's name and the time slot. The email could read:

Thank you for signing up. We'll meet about Alex at Tuesday 3:30 pm.

If this time no longer works, reply to this email and we'll find another.

Two families can still pick the same slot. Say you received their sign-up, and confirm the time yourself if slots can clash.

The confirmation guide walks through each screen, from creating the rule to sending yourself a test.

Before you send the link

  1. Fill in the form yourself with your own email address.
  2. In Formbell, preview the email with that response and save the rule.
  3. Turn alerts on and send yourself a test. On your first rule you may see Preview and test instead. Choose it to turn alerts on and send the test in one step.
  4. Check that the email arrived the way you expect, then share the form.

Sent in the send log means Google accepted the email for sending. It doesn't tell you whether it reached the inbox. Google's daily sending limits apply, which matters for a large class.
