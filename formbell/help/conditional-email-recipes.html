Formbell

3 answer-based email recipes for Google Forms™

In each recipe, Formbell emails someone automatically, but only when an answer matches. You add a few questions to your form, then create one rule in Formbell.

On the free plan you can run 1 rule at a time across your Google account, with every condition type. Running several routes at once needs Pro.

1. Refund requests go to billing

Your billing inbox gets refund requests, and nothing else.

Add a required multiple-choice question called Request type with Refund, Sales and Support. Add Order number (short answer, required), Name and Message.

In Formbell, create a rule for when Request type is Refund, and send it to your billing address. Put the order number in the subject and the name, order number and message in the body with Insert answer.

The email goes to your own inbox, so you don't need to collect respondent emails. Formbell only sends the email. You still approve and process the refund yourself.

2. Confirm event registrations

When someone says yes, they get an email that names the session they picked.

Add a required multiple-choice question called Attendance with Yes and No, plus Name and Session. If your form doesn't collect email addresses, also add a required short-answer Email question with email validation.

In Formbell, create a rule for when Attendance is Yes, and send it to the person who filled in the form. Choose Email collected by Google Forms if your form collects emails. If collection is off or Formbell can't check it, choose your Email question instead.

In the email, say that you received their registration. Don't say a seat or payment is confirmed, because the form can't check that.

3. Send each inquiry to the right person

Your sales team gets sales questions, and your support team gets support questions.

Add a required multiple-choice question called Request type with Sales, Support and Billing, plus Name and Message. Then create one rule per route, each with its own address.

On Free you can run one route at a time. To run all three, you need Pro: $4/month or $29/year, taxes included. Keep Request type a single-choice question. If you use checkboxes with contains or is any of, one response can match several rules, and Formbell sends one email per matching rule.

Try it before real responses arrive

  1. Submit a response that matches the rule. Use your own address wherever an email is needed.
  2. Open the rule, preview the email with that response, and save.
  3. On Free, if another rule is already running, choose which one runs.
  4. Turn alerts on, then click Send me a test. Formbell sends the test only to you.
  5. When real responses come in, check the send log. Sent means Google accepted the email for sending. It doesn't tell you whether the email reached the inbox.

Each rule has one condition and one recipient address, with no CC or BCC. Google's daily sending limits apply. These recipes don't come with ready-made Formbell rules, so you create the rule in your own form.
