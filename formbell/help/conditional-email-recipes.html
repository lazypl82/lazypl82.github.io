Formbell

3 answer-based email recipes for Google Forms™

In each recipe, Formbell emails someone automatically, but only when an answer matches. You build the form, then add one rule in Formbell.

On the free plan, you can run 1 rule at a time across your Google account, and you get every condition type. To run several routes at once, you need Pro.

Refund requests go to billing

Your billing inbox gets refund requests, and nothing else.

Form questions

  • Request typeMultiple choice, required · Refund / Sales / Support
  • Order numberShort answer, required
  • NameShort answer
  • MessageParagraph

Formbell rule

WhenRequest type is Refund ↓ Emailbilling@yourcompany.com
  • Subject: Refund request for Order number
  • Message: Name, Order number and Message, added with Insert answer

You don't need to collect respondent emails, because the email goes to your own inbox. Formbell only sends the email. You still approve and process the refund yourself.

Confirm event registrations

When someone says yes, they get an email that names the session they picked.

Form questions

  • AttendanceMultiple choice, required · Yes / No
  • NameShort answer, required
  • SessionMultiple choice or dropdown, required
  • EmailShort answer with email validation · only if you don't use collected email

Formbell rule

WhenAttendance is Yes ↓ EmailThe person who filled in the form
  • Subject: Your registration for Session
  • Message: greet them by Name and confirm you received their registration

If your form collects emails, pick Email collected by Google Forms. If email collection is off or can't be checked, pick your Email question. Say you received the registration, not that a seat or payment is confirmed.

Send each inquiry to the right person

Your sales team gets sales questions, and your support team gets support questions.

Form questions

  • Request typeMultiple choice, required · Sales / Support / Billing
  • NameShort answer
  • MessageParagraph, required

Formbell rules · one per route

Request type is Sales→sales@yourcompany.com
Request type is Support→support@yourcompany.com
Request type is Billing→billing@yourcompany.com

On Free, you can run one route at a time. To run all three, you need Pro: $4/month or $29/year, taxes included. Keep Request type as a single-choice question. If you use checkboxes with contains or is any of, one response can match several rules, and Formbell sends one email per matching rule.

Try it before real responses arrive

  1. Submit a response that matches the rule. Use your own address wherever an email is needed.
  2. Open the rule, preview the email with that response, and save.
  3. On Free, if another rule is already running, choose which one runs.
  4. Turn alerts on, then click Send me a test. Formbell sends the test only to you.
  5. When real responses come in, check the send log. Sent means Google accepted the email for sending. It doesn't tell you whether the email reached the inbox.

Limits: one condition per rule, one address per rule (no CC or BCC), and Google's daily sending limits. These recipes don't come with ready-made Formbell rules, so you create the rule in your own form.
