How to choose an email add-on for Google Forms

Most of these add-ons send an email when someone submits your form. They differ in when they send, who they send to, how much you can send for free, and what you can check afterwards.

First, check what Google Forms already does

Google Forms can email you when a new response arrives, and it can send respondents a copy of their answers. If that's all you need, you don't need an add-on. Google's help page shows where both settings are.

You need an add-on when the email should depend on an answer, go to someone other than you, or say something in your own words.

Five questions to ask

  1. Does the email depend on an answer? For example, only refund requests go to billing. Check whether the free plan includes conditions, or only a paid plan.
  2. Who gets it? One address per email, several addresses, CC and BCC, or an address the respondent typed in.
  3. How much can you send for free? Look for a daily or monthly email cap and a limit on how many notifications you can set up.
  4. Can you see what happened? When an email doesn't arrive, you'll want a record of what was sent and why something failed.
  5. What does the plan you need cost? Compare the yearly price of the plan that covers your answers to questions 1 to 3.

Five add-ons, checked on 13 November 2026

We read each add-on's own Marketplace listing and pricing pages. We didn't buy or test the other add-ons, so "not stated" means we couldn't confirm it, not that the feature is missing. Prices change, so check before you buy.

Several add-ons have similar names. Before you install, check the developer name on the Marketplace listing.

Which one fits

If you want one answer to email one person, and a free plan that includes conditions, Formbell, Form Notify and Form Mailer all qualify. Formbell's free plan runs one rule at a time, so compare that with the other free limits for your form.

If one email should reach several people, or you need CC and BCC, choose an add-on that lists them. Formbell sends each rule to one address.

If you need to check later whether an email went out, look for a send record. Formbell shows Sent, Failed or Unknown for recorded attempts. Sent means Google accepted the email, not that it reached the inbox.

If you only need a copy of each response, try Google Forms' own settings or Google's free Form Notifications first.
