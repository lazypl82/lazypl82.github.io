Considering an alternative to Digital Inspiration’s Email Notifications for Google Forms? Compare the price, free allowance and conditions for your first email rule.

Written by Lazy Labs, the maker of Formbell. We checked the official pages linked below on November 11, 2026. This is a documentation comparison, not a hands-on test of Digital Inspiration’s app. Prices and features can change.

If you only need an email telling you that a response arrived, first consider Google Forms’ built-in response notifications. An add-on is useful when you need personalized content or answer-based routing.

Where another app may fit better

  • More conditions in one rule: Formbell uses one condition per rule. Digital Inspiration advertises AND/OR and regex in its paid offering. [2]
  • Other channels and attachments: Formbell has no native Slack or SMS notifications and no file attachments. Digital Inspiration documents integrations and paid attachment/SMS features, plus CC/BCC in paid plans. Formbell sends to one address per rule and has no CC/BCC fields. [2] [3]
  • One active rule on Free: Formbell runs one selected rule across your account; Pro is needed to run more. Digital Inspiration’s Free edition also limits notifications. Neither free plan should be read as unlimited rules. [1] [3]

When Formbell fits

Consider Formbell when one answer-based rule, a personalized email, previewing and an explicit send log cover your workflow. Every condition type is available on Free. Accepted means Google accepted the request, not that it reached the inbox.
