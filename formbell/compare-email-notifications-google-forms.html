Formbell vs Email Notifications for Google Forms

Both are Google Forms add-ons that email people when a response arrives. This page compares their plans and features using each maker's published pages.

Written by Lazy Labs, the maker of Formbell. We compared the official pages listed under Sources on November 12, 2026. We did not test Digital Inspiration's app hands-on. Prices and features can change, so check the sources before you decide.

If you only need an email saying that a response arrived, Google Forms' built-in response notifications may be enough. An add-on helps when the email should depend on the answers or include them.

A free rule count and a free email count measure different things, so the free plans are not directly comparable. The data row summarizes each maker's own statements, not an independent security review.

Email Notifications for Google Forms may fit better if

  • one rule needs several conditions combined with AND/OR, or a regular expression.
  • you need CC or BCC, file attachments, or SMS.

Formbell may fit better if

  • one condition per rule covers your routing, and you want conditions on the free plan.
  • you want a lower Pro price: $29 per year, taxes included.

In Formbell you can preview the email with a recent response and send a test to your own address. The send log shows whether a recorded send was Sent, Failed (with a reason) or Unknown. Sent means Google accepted the email for sending. It does not confirm inbox delivery.
