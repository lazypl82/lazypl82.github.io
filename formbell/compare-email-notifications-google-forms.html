Formbell vs Email Notifications for Google Forms

A comparison with Digital Inspiration, Form Notify and Form Mailer, focused on the first email rule you need.

Written by Lazy Labs, the maker of Formbell. We checked the official pages linked below on November 11, 2026. This is a documentation comparison, not a hands-on test of the other apps. Prices and features can change.

If you only need an email telling you that a response arrived, first consider Google Forms’ built-in response notifications. An add-on is useful when you need personalized content or answer-based routing.

Where another app may fit better

  • More conditions in one rule: Formbell uses one condition per rule. Digital Inspiration advertises AND/OR and regex; Form Mailer documents AND/OR and additional text operators. [2] [9]
  • Other channels and attachments: Formbell has no native Slack or SMS notifications and no file attachments. Digital Inspiration documents integrations and paid attachment/SMS features. Form Mailer supports form-upload attachments and CC/BCC. Formbell sends to one address per rule and has no CC/BCC fields. [2] [3] [8]
  • Automatic recovery after quota exhaustion: Form Mailer says it queues emails for the next day. Formbell does not promise that recovery: inspect a retained failed entry and manually resend when offered. [8] Formbell resend guidance.
  • A free app with multiple rules: Form Mailer documents multiple rules and is listed as free. Formbell’s free allowance runs one selected rule; Pro is needed to run more. [7] [9]

When Formbell fits

Consider Formbell when one answer-based rule, a personalized email, previewing and an explicit send log cover your workflow. Every condition type is available on Free. Accepted means Google accepted the request, not that it reached the inbox.
