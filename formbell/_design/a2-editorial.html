Recipes

Three forms that email the right person, and only them

Each recipe is a few Google Forms™ questions and one Formbell rule. Formbell sends the email automatically, but only when an answer matches.

On the free plan you can run one rule at a time across your Google account. Running several routes at once needs Pro.

Recipe 1

Refund requests go to billing

Your billing inbox gets refund requests, and nothing else.

You'll need

  • Request typemultiple choice: Refund, Sales, Support
  • Order numbershort answer
  • Nameshort answer
  • Messageparagraph

The rule

When Request type is Refund, email your billing address.

Put the order number in the subject. Add the name, order number and message to the body with Insert answer.

Formbell only sends the email. You still approve and process the refund yourself.

Recipe 2

Confirm event registrations

When someone says yes, they get an email that names the session they picked.

You'll need

  • Attendancemultiple choice: Yes, No
  • Nameshort answer
  • Sessionmultiple choice or dropdown

The rule

When Attendance is Yes, email the person who filled in the form.

If your form collects email addresses, choose the email Google Forms collects. If it doesn't, add a short-answer Email question with email validation and choose that instead.

Say you received their registration. Don't promise a seat or a payment the form can't check.

Recipe 3

Send each inquiry to the right person

Sales questions go to sales, and support questions go to support.

You'll need

  • Request typemultiple choice: Sales, Support, Billing
  • Nameshort answer
  • Messageparagraph

The rules

  1. When Request type is Sales, email your sales address.
  2. When Request type is Support, email your support address.
  3. When Request type is Billing, email your billing address.

On Free you can run one route at a time. All three together needs Pro, $4 a month or $29 a year, taxes included.
