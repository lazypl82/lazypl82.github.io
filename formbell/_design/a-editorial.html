Recipes

Three Google Forms™ that email the right person, and only them

Each one is a handful of questions and a single Formbell rule. Formbell sends the email automatically, but only when an answer matches.

On the free plan you can run one rule at a time across your Google account. Running several routes at once needs Pro.

Recipe 1

Refund requests go to billing

Your billing inbox gets refund requests, and nothing else.

Add a required multiple-choice question, Request type, with Refund, Sales and Support. Then add Order number, Name and Message. In Formbell, create a rule for when Request type is Refund and send it to your billing address.

Formbell only sends the email. You still approve and process the refund yourself.

Recipe 2

Confirm event registrations

When someone says yes, they get an email that names the session they picked.

Add Attendance (Yes or No), Name and Session. Create a rule for when Attendance is Yes, and send it to the person who filled in the form, using the email Google Forms collects.

Say you received the registration. Don't promise a seat or a payment the form can't check.

Recipe 3

Send each inquiry to the right person

Your sales team gets sales questions, and your support team gets support questions. Make one rule per route, each with its own address.

On Free you can run one route at a time. All three together needs Pro, $4 a month or $29 a year, taxes included.
